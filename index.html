About

FluidLab allows you to simulate 2D smoke and fire inside of After Effects. Just apply the FluidLab effect to a layer, assign fuel, collision, and light layers, and watch it flow. Everything is keyframable, and it animates fast on the CPU, or optionally on GPU.

  • Motion blurStreaks follow the simulation itself, using your comp's shutter or your own
  • CollisionsTurn on collisions to interact with the smoke
  • LightingTurn on the optional lighting system and add depth to your smoke
  • 2D or native lightsUse the built-in FluidLab 2D lights, or native After Effects lights
  • Built-in stylesCustomize your smoke with style effects including ASCII, Grid, Flow, Dither, and more
  • GPU-acceleratedFor fast simulations

Setup

  1. Install. Run the installer for your system. It needs an administrator password, since After Effects plug-ins live in a shared folder.
  2. Start (or restart) After Effects.
  3. Apply the effect. Make a solid the size of your comp, then apply Effect → FluidLab → FluidLab. Smoke starts from the built-in emitter right away.
  4. Choose your layers. Set them under Fuel 1–3, Collision, and Burst 1–2. The layers you pick can stay hidden.
  5. Pick a look. Choose one from the Preset menu at the top of the effect: Large presets suit backgrounds, Small ones give finer detail for text.

If the installer won't open

The installers aren't signed yet.

macOS: if the installer says it can't be opened, right-click it and choose Open. On macOS 15 and later, open System Settings → Privacy & Security instead and click Open Anyway.

Windows: if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Install location and uninstalling

Where it installs

macOS: to uninstall, delete this folder.

/Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/Plug-ins/7.0/MediaCore/FluidLab

Windows: to uninstall, use Settings → Apps.

C:\Program Files\Adobe\Common\Plug-ins\7.0\MediaCore\FluidLab

Requirements

After Effects on macOS 12 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel) or 64-bit Windows. Developed and tested on After Effects 2026; earlier versions haven't been tested. The Windows version is newer and has had less testing; its GPU mode (DirectX 11, which needs a GPU with feature level 11.1) is new in 0.1.31. Everything runs locally: no accounts and no network calls.
